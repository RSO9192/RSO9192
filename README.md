# 👋 Hi, I'm Riccardo

I work at the intersection of **climate science, data, AI, and sustainable development**, using quantitative and computational methods to support **risk assessment, risk management, and decision-making**.

My work combines climate, geospatial, socio-economic, and other data sources to understand risks, identify where they matter most, and support practical responses across different countries and contexts.

---

## 🌍 Current Focus

* **Risk assessment & management** — combining different sources of evidence to understand, map, and manage complex risks
* **Climate services** — improving access to and use of climate information for planning and decision-making
* **CAVA framework** — supporting climate risk assessment and adaptation planning through integrated climate data and analytical workflows
* **Data & AI** — using geospatial analysis, data pipelines, and AI to support analysis, evidence synthesis, and decision-making
* **Open & reproducible approaches** — developing methods that can be transparently applied and adapted across different contexts
